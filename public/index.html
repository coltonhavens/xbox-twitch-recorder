01 / THE WORKFLOW

Stream once.
Plan to save the whole session.

The goal is a scheduled cloud worker that finds completed saved broadcasts and uploads each full video to the owner’s Drive.

01

Go live from Xbox

Broadcast gameplay and microphone audio to Twitch, with saved broadcasts enabled. The archive uses the audio in the saved video.

02

Find the completed VOD

A scheduled cloud worker checks that the saved broadcast is complete, then downloads its full video and audio.

03

Keep it in your Drive

With the owner’s Google authorization, the job would upload one verified MP4 to the existing Google Drive folder.

02 / DESIGNED FOR PERSONAL USE

Your streams.
Your destination.

The planned service is for the owner’s Twitch account and Google Drive. It is not a public recording platform or a way to archive other people’s broadcasts.

Follow development on GitHub →
◉

Audio follows the source

The intended archive includes game and microphone audio present in the saved Twitch video. It cannot recover missing, muted, or excluded audio.

☁

A cloud workflow is the goal

Future processing would run in the cloud so the home computer could stay off. A scheduled, self-stopping cloud VM worker is prepared for review; cloud infrastructure and account authorization are not active.

→

Availability has limits

Twitch VOD availability, muted source audio, playback restrictions, and Drive storage limits affect archiving. Complete recovery is not guaranteed.

03 / CLEAR ABOUT DATA

Know what access would mean.

This site does not connect accounts or process recordings. Read about current website data and the intended handling of permissions, tokens, and files.
